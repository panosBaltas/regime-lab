# Diversifying the carry model

20 October 2026. Native inverse carry has standard daily excess Sharpe 3.81 in 2024 onward, but monthly aggregation gives approximately 1.31 and a 28-day Newey–West risk diagnostic approximately 1.37. Funding is serially persistent; the daily number alone does not establish a robust annual Sharpe of two. These results are now inspected research data.

Combine the development/2023-selected native carry sleeve and the independently development/2023-selected ETF sleeve, preserving each sleeve's real trading fees. Three fixed allocation rules: (1) equal-risk weights based on pre-2023 monthly excess returns, (2) equal-risk weights from the previous 12 completed monthly observations, and (3) nonnegative mean–variance weights from the previous 12 completed months, with 50% covariance shrinkage, a 50% shrink of each mean toward the cross-sleeve mean, and individual weights bounded to [10%,90%]. Rebalance monthly with a 10-bps charge on capital moved between venues/sleeves. No extra borrowing or full-sample optimizer.

Select one rule using only 2023 monthly excess Sharpe. Evaluate all three in 2024 onward and label this evaluation reused context. Report daily and monthly Sharpe together, serial dependence, rolling/yearly performance, and a hypothetical venue-loss stress. Do not select the best evaluation Sharpe as the model. Implementation review corrected allocation timing so that a newly filled weight earns only subsequent returns. A capital transfer additionally pays underlying execution (19 bps carry, 5 bps ETF) and holds moved cash idle for two calendar days. Final transfers and the ETF exit are charged; the carry input already contains its own liquidation. This is an asynchronous return-sleeve allocation model, with direct multi-venue execution still requiring forward validation.

For common calendar marks, map ETF wealth to the most recent available US opening NAV, shifted to the following UTC midnight; never move a future US opening return earlier in the day. Carry uses native 00:00 UTC NAV. Weekends carry the last observed ETF sleeve wealth. BIL benchmark wealth uses the same calendar alignment. Monthly allocations use strictly earlier completed returns; fill one calendar day after the allocation observation. This creates an explicitly asynchronous daily portfolio, so the primary combined result is the monthly measure and daily Sharpe is secondary. No hypothetical perfect synchronous ETF/crypto executions.
