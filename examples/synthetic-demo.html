Funding carry: from returns to an account

Synthetic demonstration — no evidence of profitability. No Sharpe target is an acceptance test. The new execution variant has its own results.

The reference policy is rolling_risk. Both controls use the same account engine. Funding accrues to old whole-contract holdings; transfer cash earns zero; positions fill only at subsequent executable observations.

Monthly Sharpe uses complete calendar months between consecutive midnight month-start NAVs; boundary partial months are excluded. The pointwise three-month block-bootstrap intervals in uncertainty.json do not correct research selection.

Where the dollars came from

Funding, collateral price movements, inverse-contract PnL, ETF PnL, quote-currency changes, execution basis/spread and fees reconcile to total account PnL at every event. Execution PnL is computed independently from each trade's fill price versus its mark; an unexplained cash change fails the run.

Interpretation and remaining limits

Daily and monthly Sharpe use matched BIL excess returns and different aggregation. The 28-day HAC value is a dependence diagnostic. ETF marks and crypto marks remain asynchronous. Historical ETF positions are fractional adjusted total-return units; raw shares, corporate actions and actual broker fills require different data. Ending NAV includes open positions and is not a liquidation guarantee.

Two business days excludes weekends but does not reconstruct bank holidays, withdrawal queues or outages. The maintenance buffer is a research constraint, not an historical exchange-tier simulator. Fees are stated assumptions. Venue default, ADL, custody loss, historical order-book depth and executable capacity are not estimated by this backtest.

Audit files

run.json, daily_nav.csv, per-policy event ledgers, orders, decisions and daily holdings, pnl_attribution.csv, yearly_metrics.csv and reconciliation.json make the accounting inspectable.
