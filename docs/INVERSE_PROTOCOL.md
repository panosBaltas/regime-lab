# Coin-collateralized funding carry extension

20 October 2026, before native COIN-M strategy scores. The linear carry and ETF results have now been inspected. Their market context is reused; the same calendar dates are not an independent new holdout.

The linear hedge used 70% spot and a separate 30% cash margin reserve. A genuinely different instrument can use the purchased coin as collateral: short BTCUSD_PERP or ETHUSD_PERP, whose inverse contract is settled in BTC or ETH. Implement its reciprocal-price payoff, not a relabeled linear return or a favorable change in the Sharpe benchmark.

For short USD face `F`, coin PnL between marks is `F*(1/new_mark - 1/old_mark)`. Funding adds `F/funding_mark * funding_rate` coins. Coin wallet plus unrealized coin PnL is marked into USD using spot. Futures reductions realize reciprocal-price PnL; increases add a new entry-price term. A matched initial short offsets the collateral coin's directional exposure. Subsequent funding leaves a small coin delta that can be hedged by adjusting actual contract face. Fees and contract granularity matter.

- Fixed BTC and ETH sleeves, initially equal dollar capital. $100,000 illustrative account; BTC contract face $100 and ETH $10, rounded to whole contracts. Each sleeve buys coin with at most 98% of its own cash, pays 12 bps one-way spot cost and 7 bps one-way futures cost, and posts the coin to the native futures account. No borrowed spot, no external cash yield, and no separate fictitious dollar collateral account. Assets' dollar weights drift with their own returns; no free inter-sleeve rebalancing.
- Actual native COIN-M hourly trades, marks and funding archives from September 2020 through September 2026. Use contemporaneous spot USDT prices converted to USD with daily USDT/USD observations; retain all basis changes. Use archived funding-hour mark if the API event mark is unavailable, explicitly audited.
- Reuse the 18 prespecified passive/positive/net-cost/OU candidate rules and windows for native settled funding. Decisions use strictly earlier data. Monthly/weekly decisions at 00:00 execute at 01:00. Same funding before-fill convention. Hedge adjustments trade contract face, not the entire collateral coin holding every day.
- An active sleeve adjusts the short to neutralize wallet-minus-entry coin exposure, accounting for its actual futures fee and contract rounding. An inactive sleeve closes contracts at trade price, realizes coin PnL, sells actual remaining coins, and holds quote cash earning zero. No credit for unearned interest. Test native coin-equity margin against 10% of inverse short coin notional at each hourly high and low. Any breach is reported and disqualifies selection.
- Development November 2020–2022, validation 2023; freeze selection by maximum worst-period net excess Sharpe among active, margin-valid candidates, then reveal 2024+. Cash benchmark and 365-day annualization remain unchanged. Report full catalog and periods. Do not claim the selection is independent of earlier research on these dates.
- Stress double costs, four-hour fills, 90% funding receipts with all negative payments retained, contract granularity, and a hypothetical 10% venue-loss shock. Fit no parameter to make the evaluation Sharpe equal two. Preserve all preceding failed strategies.
