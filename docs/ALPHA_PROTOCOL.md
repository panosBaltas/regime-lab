# Broader alpha research: carry and diversified trend

Written 20 October 2026 before viewing new crypto-strategy returns. The previous stock candidates failed. They remain preserved. This study targets net excess Sharpe 2 without conditioning a strategy on its eventual test returns.

## Economic hypotheses

1. Perpetual funding compensates the spot-long/perpetual-short side when leveraged demand is predominantly long. Delta hedging removes most coin-price exposure; basis changes, negative funding, fees, collateral, stablecoin and venue risk remain. Pure Brownian motion does not supply a return forecast. A mean-reverting basis can provide a separate, estimated spread forecast.
2. Slow diversified time-series momentum and volatility-managed portfolios may supply returns from a different source. Use economically specified asset classes and previous-price signals, rather than selecting companies after seeing their returns.

Sources: [BIS, Crypto carry](https://www.bis.org/publ/work1087.htm); [Binance public data](https://github.com/binance/binance-public-data); [Binance funding-arbitrage mechanics](https://www.binance.com/en-AE/support/faq/detail/f330e17d6fc04679b9b21d6f9350e787); [AQR, A Century of Evidence on Trend-Following Investing](https://www.aqr.com/insights/research/journal-article/a-century-of-evidence-on-trend-following-investing); [Moreira–Muir, Volatility Managed Portfolios](https://www.nber.org/papers/w22208).

## Crypto data and accounting

- Fixed initial universe BTCUSDT and ETHUSDT: two established assets, with no selection by this study's returns. Free Binance monthly archives, January 2020 through September 2026: hourly spot trades, perpetual trades, perpetual mark prices, and all actual funding settlements. Cache downloads and checksums; refuse missing internal hours or missing price observations at funding events. Log all failed months.
- Positions are actual equal-quantity spot-long/perpetual-short pairs. Funding uses held short quantity times the event mark price times the actual settled funding rate. Both spot and perpetual basis PnL are marked. Never equate funding receipts with total strategy profit.
- Use only settled funding and completed price bars before a decision. Decide at 00:00 UTC using observations strictly earlier, execute at 01:00 UTC. Funding at a fill timestamp belongs to the previous holding, not a newly opened trade. Delayed execution is stressed separately.
- Total spot purchases initially at most 70% of NAV; the remaining 30% is cash collateral. No interest on exchange cash, no spot borrowing, no reverse carry, and no assumption that short proceeds supply free financing. Rebalance quantities on scheduled decisions, rather than marking fixed weights as self-rebalancing for free.
- Record cash, marked spot value, perpetual mark-to-market, funding, turnover, fees, and collateral headroom. Intrahour perpetual high prices test a 10%-of-short-notional maintenance buffer against isolated cash collateral. An unsafe proposal is reduced; an in-position breach is a reported failure, not silently ignored or treated as a normal successful trade.
- Primary one-way costs, including slippage: spot 12 bps, perpetual 7 bps. Stress double costs. Quote returns are USDT; report USD conversion using dated USDT/USD daily opens separately when available, and show a discrete depeg/venue-loss stress. Neither mark-to-market Sharpe nor a stress test prices an exchange default probability.
- Benchmark: matched BIL total returns across calendar intervals; weekend returns apportioned for benchmark reporting only. Strategy decisions may use only previously available cash-return estimates. Report both zero-cash and excess Sharpe, with 365-day annualization for daily crypto/calendar returns. Do not mix 252 trading-day and 365 calendar-day scales.

## Candidate family and validation

- Initial funding-carry family: funding estimation windows 3/7/30 days; scheduled rebalances every 7/30 days; either positive-funding gate or a financing-and-roundtrip-cost hurdle. Include passive buy-spot/short-perpetual carry as a control. Allocate equally across eligible pairs; total spot allocation 70%. Decisions are deterministic functions of prior observations.
- Before performance evaluation, specify four additional OU-gated candidates: funding windows 7/30 days and rebalance intervals 7/30 days, with the net-cost hurdle augmented by expected basis convergence from a trailing 30-day hourly AR(1). Include both 7/30-day passive controls, for 18 candidates total. An hourly collateral guard uses the previous completed hour: if cash collateral falls below 30% of marked short notional, reduce pair quantities at the next hourly open to at most 65% of NAV in spot. This is a causal risk trade with both-leg fees, not an intrabar stop with assumed perfect fills. A subsequent intrahour maintenance breach invalidates the candidate.
- Funding API event marks are used where supplied. Early missing event marks use the archived hour-opening mark at that funding timestamp and are counted in the audit. Funding rates must reconcile against the ZIP archive. The archive and API are downloaded independently.
- Data validation before any performance inspection found missing mark-price archive days and 31 spot hours shared by both assets. Recover mark archive gaps from the official historical mark API and retain a repair audit. Spot venue halts remain untradable; use the last completed close only for intermediate valuation, record staleness, and delay pending fills. Require fresh held prices at every reported 00:00 UTC daily NAV. Do not remove bad trading days or manufacture fills during a halt.
- A separately identified basis-mean-reversion gate can fit an AR(1)/Ornstein–Uhlenbeck approximation on trailing hourly log basis; estimate parameters with past data only. No future fitted mean or volatility.
- Crypto development 2020–2022, validation 2023, evaluation 2024 through September 2026. Do not inspect evaluation strategy scores before freezing the chosen specification. General macro context was seen in previous studies, so do not call this a pristine project-wide holdout. Keep every tried candidate, including failures and controls. If later experiments use an inspected period, label that period reused research data.
- Primary selector uses development and validation scores, favoring stable net excess Sharpe and adequate activity over a single favorable year. Freeze it before loading evaluation results. Report the selected method, the entire catalog, yearly scores, execution/cost stresses, and uncertainty. Report an ex-post best candidate as ex post, never as the selected method.
- ETF candidates use lagged close signals and next-open execution, diversified asset-class risk allocation, slow trend horizons, and fixed cost conventions. Their previously inspected market history is reused evidence. Keep their selection separate from any genuinely new crypto evidence.
- Macro catalog before scoring: existing fixed 12-ETF panel (five equity, four bond, two real-asset and one dollar exposure). Long-only and long/short trend at 63/126/252 sessions; four-speed 21/63/126/252 continuous trend; risk parity; inverse variance. Each rebalances every 5/21 sessions. Add top-1/3/5 slow relative momentum, weighted 1/3/6/12-month fast momentum, and fast momentum with defensive breadth, for 29 candidates. Use 50%-diagonal-shrunk trailing 63-session covariance, 10% volatility ceiling, gross target <=1, 5 bps one-way fees, 2% annual ETF short borrow and a conservative cash policy. Select by worst pre-2023/2023 excess Sharpe, then reveal 2024+ outcomes. All ETF context is previously inspected research history.
- Any cross-strategy combination uses training-period or rolling past covariance and returns; no full-sample Sharpe maximization. No leverage multiplier is presented as improving an unchanged strategy's excess Sharpe.
- Save exact inputs, configurations, source hashes, selection dates/scores, trade ledgers, numerical checks, and all trial results. Use daily block-bootstrap intervals as diagnostics and acknowledge selection uncertainty. Target attainment is a result to verify, not a rule for inventing favorable metrics.
